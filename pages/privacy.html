Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 5, 2026

At Desert Dreamers Camp, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website and booking engine.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including but not limited to your name, contact information (email address, telephone number), check-in and check-out dates, guest preferences, and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to process bookings, send confirmation emails, communicate with you regarding your stay, respond to enquiries, and comply with legal requirements. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security & PCI Compliance

We take data security very seriously. All payment transactions are handled via Razorpay, our PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. Card numbers and sensitive financial information are never processed or stored on our servers or databases.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at reservations@desertdreamerscamp.com.
