Signature Experience

Sunset Camel Safari

✦

No trip to Jaisalmer is complete without swaying rhythmically atop a decorated desert camel into the untouched ripples of the Sam dunes. As the sun sets, the desert glows in shades of copper, amber, and molten gold.

  • ✦ Guided ride into secluded high dunes
  • ✦ Spectacular golden sunset photography point
  • ✦ Evening tea & hot pakoras served on dunes
  • ✦ Included complimentary in all camp stay packages

4x4 Thar Jeep Dune Bashing

✦
